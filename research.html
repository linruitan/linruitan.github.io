MindCare: A Hybrid Multi-Agent Architecture for Stress-Aware Conversational Support

Linrui Tan, Shouyu Wang, Junen Xiao, Yifan Zhou, Akane Sano

Rice University, Computational Wellbeing Lab

A stress-aware conversational agent: a calibrated SVC stress classifier routes each message to one of two Gemini-based assistants, a grounding companion for stressful moments and an encouraging one for calm ones, and every turn is logged for analysis.

Fiber Optical Parametric Amplification of Low-Photon-Flux Microscopy Signals

J. Demas, L. Tan, S. Ramachandran

Boston University, High Dimensional Photonics Lab

Fiber-based parametric optical amplification paired with conventional high-bandwidth photodiodes overcomes the sensitivity–bandwidth trade-off in low-photon-flux detection, reaching more than 10× higher sensitivity at more than 10× higher bandwidths.

Angle-Insensitive Plasmonic Metasurfaces for Spectral Imaging

Linrui Tan

University of Rochester, with Prof. Cotrufo

Designed and simulated angle-insensitive plasmonic metasurfaces, exploring how nanostructure geometry and materials shape the transmission spectra of 100 topology-optimized units (RCWA, 450–750 nm, 0° and 15° incidence).

Electro-Optic Modulators for the RGB Color Space

Linrui Tan

University of Rochester, Laboratory of Nanophotonics (Prof. Qiang Lin)

Developed and analyzed electro-optic modulators, optimizing drive voltages and color coverage to determine Vπ at 450, 520 and 633 nm for high-definition, wide-gamut EO displays.
