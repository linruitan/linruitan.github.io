01About

Seeing more with light.

Welcome to my webpage! I am a PhD student at Rice University in Prof. Ashok Veeraraghavan's group, working on optics and computational imaging.

I was an undergraduate at the University of Rochester, working on metaoptics, fiber optics, and traditional imaging. Outside the lab I translate the Lotus Sutra, take photographs, and study classical Chinese medicine.
